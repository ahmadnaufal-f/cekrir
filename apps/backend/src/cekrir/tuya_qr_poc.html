Connect a Tuya camera

Scan a Cloud Project QR code and check whether the project can access your camera.

Scan the Tuya QR code

In the Tuya console, open your Cloud Project. Go to Devices → Link App Account → Add App Account → Tuya App Account Authorization.

Scan the code in Tuya or Smart Life, then finish linking the account in the console.

Verify the linked camera

Copy the UID from the linked account in the console. In Tuya, find the camera’s Virtual ID under Device Information.
